# NIS2 tabletop drill (90 min)

Roles: facilitator, incident lead, comms, legal/DPO, management decision-maker, IT/SOC, scribe. Clock: simulated, starts at "awareness".

| T | Inject | Decision required | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Brief, roles, rules (no blame, simulated only) | — | All roles present |
| 0:10 | Detection signal on a critical service | Severity; is it potentially significant? | Decision + rationale logged |
| 0:25 | Scope grows / supplier involved | Send 24 h early warning? Who signs off? | Draft early warning ready, owner named |
| 0:45 | Personal data possibly affected; customers asking | GDPR interplay; customer comms | Comms draft; DPO engaged |
| 1:05 | Containment and recovery options | Restore from backup? | RTO/RPO checked against plan |
| 1:20 | 72 h notification | Draft content | Template filled to ≥80% |
| 1:20-1:30 | Hot debrief | Gaps, owners, dates | Actions in risk backlog; evidence logged |
